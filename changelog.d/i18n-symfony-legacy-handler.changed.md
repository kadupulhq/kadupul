- Legacy pages now resolve `__()` and its variants through Symfony Translation by default, with compiled catalogues cached in `var/cache/legacy-translations`. The text is unchanged. "Use the PHP GetText Handler" under Settings > General, or `$i18n_handler = 1;` in `include/config.php`, selects the previous handler; a saved handler choice is now honoured. An unreadable language file shows its text in English and logs a warning instead of ending the request.
