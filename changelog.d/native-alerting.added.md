- Add the native alerting tables `alert_rule`, `alert_rule_state`, `alert_incident` and `alert_notification` in the 1.2.40 upgrade step, with DBAL stores for rules, rule state, incidents and the evaluation cursor. The step creates tables only; nothing is evaluated or sent until alerting is enabled. A rule's state and incident change commit together, an incident whose data source, device, RRD data source or rule is deleted ends as `orphaned` without a notification, a rule that stays `unknown` is not rewritten each run, and an observation more than 60 seconds ahead of the clock is `unknown`.
- Queue alert notifications in `alert_notification` and deliver them separately. An incident that opens or recovers queues its mail in the same transaction as its state and incident change, and a rule deleted mid-run writes nothing. Delivery claims each row with a lease so two deliverers never send it together, mails the administrator through the existing administrative mail path, marks a row `sent` only after the transport accepts it, retries a failure after 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes and marks the row `failed` after five attempts. The mail names the rule, series, value, threshold, transition, time and incident path, escapes device text and holds no credential or query.
